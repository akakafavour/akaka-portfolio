# Prospects — Pipeline Tracker

> Working tracker. Seeded from `prospects-seed.md` (agent research), maintained daily.
> Every row MUST have a Next Action + date. Nothing lives in memory.

## Qualification rubric (score before adding)

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Established business (20+ Google reviews / years active) | +2 |
| High-value service (hotel, real estate, clinic, law, school, architecture) | +2 |
| Terrible/dated website | +4 |
| Broken or poor mobile experience | +3 |
| No clear call-to-action above the fold | +2 |
| Slow or broken pages | +3 |
| Active on social (reachable) | +2 |

**10+ = qualified.** Contact only qualified rows. Exclude modern, well-built sites.

## Rules
- One redesign attempt per business (homepage only), posted to the gallery + sent directly.
- The message: "I redesigned your homepage — it's yours free either way. If you want the full site built, here's the price."
- 50% upfront before any build starts. No exceptions.
- Log every send in `log.md` same day.
