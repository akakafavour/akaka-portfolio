# Outreach Kit — Email First Message (redesign-first)

> **The rule:** the redesign exists before this email does. Attach 1–2 screenshots OR a link to a live one-page demo of their redesign. Never write "I'd love to redesign your site" — the redesign is already done. That's the whole move.

---

## Variant 1 — Nigerian business owner

**Subject options (pick one):**
- `[Business]'s homepage, rebuilt — yours free`
- `I already redesigned your homepage (free, either way)`

**Body:**

> Hello [Name],
>
> I'm Akaka, a web designer in Port Harcourt. I build websites for [hotels / real estate firms / clinics / law firms / schools] — fast, mobile-first sites whose only job is turning visitors into calls and bookings.
>
> I looked at [Business]'s website and rebuilt your homepage. Attached. It's yours free whether we work together or not — take the design to any developer if you like.
>
> If you'd rather I build the full site: [Price], live in [Days] days. You deal with me directly, start to finish.
>
> Worth ten minutes of your time?
>
> Akaka Favour
> [Phone / WhatsApp] · [Email]

## Follow-up nudge (once, 4–5 days later)

**Subject:** `Re: [original subject]` (thread reply)

> [Name] — quick nudge on the redesign I sent. Still yours free either way.
>
> If it's a "not now", tell me and I'll stop here. If it's a "maybe", what would make it a yes?

---

### Voice notes
- Fill every bracket before sending. One email with brackets visible burns the prospect forever.
- Email is the one channel where two short paragraphs of context are acceptable — still keep it under 120 words.
- Never attach more than 2 images or the spam folder eats it. Link beats attachment when the demo is live.
- Banned words apply here too. So does "discovery call" — offer "ten minutes", not a meeting tier.
- Interested reply → `outreach/proposal-template.md`. Fifty percent upfront.
