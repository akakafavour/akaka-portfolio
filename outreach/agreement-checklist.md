# Agreement Checklist — Plain Terms

> This is NOT a legal contract and must never be presented as one. It's a one-page plain-terms checklist you and the client tick together before work starts — so the project never dies on "I thought that was included." If a client needs a formal contract, that's a lawyer's job, not a template.

**Project:** [Business] website — [Tier]
**Akaka Favour** (designer) and **[Client name], [Business]** (client) agree to the following before any work starts.

---

## Before the first naira moves

- [ ] Scope agreed in writing (this page or the proposal) — pages, features, WhatsApp button.
- [ ] Price agreed: **₦/$ [amount] for [tier]**.
- [ ] Payment schedule agreed: **50% now, 50% at launch**. No launch, no handover, no exceptions.
- [ ] Payment details confirmed (bank account / transfer route), receipts promised both ways.

## What the client provides (and by when)

- [ ] Text inputs: services/prices/menu, address, phone & WhatsApp number, social links — by **[date]**.
- [ ] Photos: real photos of the business (phone shots fine) — by **[date]**.
- [ ] Logo file if one exists — by **[date]** (no logo = clean text branding, decided now, not mid-build).
- [ ] Access: domain registrar and/or hosting login if they already own a domain — by **[date]**.
- [ ] One decision-maker who answers within 24 hours during the build. Slow answers move the launch date day-for-day.

## What Akaka delivers

- [ ] First review link by **[date]**.
- [ ] **Two rounds of revisions** included — each round is one consolidated list of changes from the client, not daily drips.
- [ ] Mobile-first build tested on small screens before launch.
- [ ] Launch on the client's domain + handover of every file, login, and account. The client owns their name, domain, content, and site.
- [ ] **[X] days of post-launch fixes** for anything broken (tier-dependent, e.g. 30 days on Full site + booking). New features after that are new quotes.

## What neither side does

- [ ] The client doesn't bring extra pages/features mid-build without a new price (agreed in writing — even a WhatsApp message counts — before the work happens).
- [ ] Akaka doesn't hold the site hostage: if payment-part-two is late, the launch waits, but the client's content and money already spent are honored — the site goes live as soon as the balance clears.
- [ ] Neither side publishes the other's private info. Client photos/content stay the client's; the build relationship may be shown as portfolio work UNLESS the client ticks the box below.

## Portfolio rights

- [ ] **Client agrees Akaka may show this project in his portfolio and case studies** (tick one): YES / NO / YES, after [date].
- [ ] If YES: any metrics or client quotes shown will be real and approved by the client first.

## If it goes wrong

- [ ] Either side can stop the project in writing. Client pays for work already done (proportional to milestones); Akaka hands over whatever exists.
- [ ] Disagreements first get one honest conversation — phone or in person — before anything else.
