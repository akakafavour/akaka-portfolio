# Outreach Kit — DM First Message (X / Facebook, redesign-first)

> **The rule:** the redesign exists first. On X and Facebook the redesign can be the DM's hook: a single screenshot, or better, a public post of the redesign that the DM references ("I posted this earlier — it's yours"). Public proof first, private pitch second. Never DM a wall of text.

---

## Variant 1 — Nigerian business owner (Facebook / X)

> Hello [Name], I'm Akaka — web designer here in Nigeria.
>
> I redesigned [Business]'s homepage (screenshot / post below). It's yours free whether we ever talk again — take it, use it, no strings.
>
> If you want the full site built: [Price], live in [Days] days, everything working on a phone.
>
> Should I send the rest?

## Variant 2 — International owner (X)

> [Name] — I rebuilt your homepage before asking permission (attached). Yours free either way; keep it or bin it.
>
> If you want the person who already did the thinking to build the full site: $[Price], [Days] days, done.
>
> Happy to explain any choice in the design.

## Follow-up nudge (once, 3 days later)

> Quick one, [Name] — did the redesign reach you? Free either way.
>
> Not interested is a fine answer. A one-word reply saves us both the mystery.

---

### Voice notes
- X DMs from new accounts land in "Message requests" — the screenshot is what gets it opened. Lead with the image, then two lines of text, then the price.
- Facebook: business Pages' inboxes are checked; personal profiles are riskier. Prefer the Page, and prefer commenting something useful on their public post BEFORE the DM (the Connection Ladder — engage first, DM second).
- The price goes IN the first message. "Depends on requirements" is how cold DMs die. One number, said plainly, builds more trust than a call.
- Never send links in the first DM — links from strangers get ignored or reported. Screenshot first, link after they reply.
- Banned words apply: no "game-changer", "crush it", "deep dive", "unleash", "elevate", "let that sink in", "in today's world", "unpopular opinion", no engagement bait.
- Reply with interest → move to WhatsApp (Nigerian) or email (international), then `outreach/proposal-template.md`. Fifty percent upfront.
