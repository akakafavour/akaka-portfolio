# Outreach Kit — WhatsApp First Message (redesign-first)

> **The rule that makes this work:** the redesign must EXIST before the message is sent. This is not a cold pitch — it's a finished piece of work being handed over. Screenshot the redesigned homepage (the portfolio's hero slider is the demo; for a real prospect, build the one-screen redesign first). Never send without it. Never invent that you've "been looking at" their business beyond what their public page shows.

**Structure of every message:** one line of context → the redesign is yours free either way → the price, said out loud → one soft question. No links except the screenshot. Short enough to read on a lock screen.

---

## Variant 1 — Nigerian business owner

**Send after attaching 1–2 screenshots of their redesigned homepage:**

> Hello [Name], I'm Akaka, a web designer in Port Harcourt.
>
> I looked at [Business]'s website and rebuilt your homepage to show you what it could be — screenshots attached.
>
> It's yours free, whether we work together or not. Take the design, give it to any developer, no hard feelings.
>
> If you'd rather I build the full site: [Price], live in [Days] days, everything on one page your customers can actually use on a phone.
>
> Worth a quick look at the rest?

## Variant 2 — International owner

**Send after attaching 1–2 screenshots:**

> Hi [Name] — Akaka here, I design and build websites for [hotels / clinics / firms] that need to look as good online as they do in person.
>
> I rebuilt your homepage. Screenshots attached — it's yours free either way. Use it, keep it, hand it to your developer.
>
> If you want me to build the full site: $[Price], live in [Days] days, mobile-first, and you talk to me directly the whole way.
>
> Happy to walk you through the thinking behind the redesign.

## Follow-up nudge (send 3–4 days later, once only)

> Just checking you saw the redesign I sent, [Name]. No pressure at all — it's yours free either way.
>
> If the timing is wrong, say the word and I'll close the file. If you have questions about the price or the timeline, I'm here.

---

### Voice notes for whoever edits before sending
- Fill every bracket. A message with brackets visible is a deleted message.
- Nigerian variant: keep it warm but straight; WhatsApp is a normal business channel here — no apologies for messaging.
- International variant: no currency confusion — quote in their currency, state timezone overlap if asked, never lead with "Nigeria" as a discount signal.
- Banned: "game-changer", "crush it", "deep dive", "unleash", "elevate", "let that sink in", "in today's world", "unpopular opinion".
- If they reply with any interest, the next step is the proposal: `outreach/proposal-template.md`. Fifty percent upfront, no exceptions.
